# Source Guide — Corporate Action and Rekind Sector Intelligence

## 1. Source tiers

### Tier 1 — Official and primary

Use to establish official status and material facts.

- IDX and OJK disclosures;
- company announcements and investor relations;
- annual/financial reports and RUPS documents;
- official ministries and regulators;
- Kementerian ESDM, Kementerian BUMN, SKK Migas, and relevant agencies;
- official entities within Pupuk Indonesia Group;
- official tender/project/contract announcements when accessible.

### Tier 2A — Indonesian media

- CNBC Indonesia;
- Kontan;
- Bisnis Indonesia;
- Katadata;
- Kompas Money/Finance;
- other credible Indonesian business media.

### Tier 2B — International media

- **CNBC International / CNBC.com**;
- Reuters;
- Bloomberg;
- Financial Times;
- other credible international energy/business media.

Tier 2 sources discover events and add context. They do not replace official
confirmation.

### Tier 3 — Sector, technical, and research

Industry associations, commodity/energy agencies, technical media, research
institutions, market reports, academic studies, and credible specialist
publications.

## 2. CNBC rules

### CNBC Indonesia

Use for Indonesian company, policy, commodity, energy, and project developments.
Cross-check material figures and official status.

### CNBC International

Search CNBC.com in English for:

- global oil, gas, LNG, power, renewable energy, fertilizer, ammonia,
  petrochemical, feedstock, commodity, and industrial investment developments;
- major global company actions affecting supply, technology, or project
  pipeline;
- policy/geopolitical events only when they have a direct sector transmission
  path.

Do not include a CNBC International story merely because it is global or
trending. It must pass the Mode K selection gate.

## 3. Search strategy

### Corporate action

1. official source for known event;
2. Indonesian media for discovery/context;
3. international media if transaction/global party requires it;
4. sector sources for background.

### Rekind Sector Brief

For each of four sectors, run separate:

- Indonesian query;
- English international query;
- official verification query for material items.

Example query dimensions, not fixed strings:

- sector + project/investment/tender + current year;
- sector + regulation/policy + current year;
- sector + price/supply/demand + current year;
- sector + technology/O&M/maintenance + current year;
- sector + Indonesia/ASEAN/global + current year.

## 4. Source metadata

Record:

- source name;
- title;
- publication date;
- event date if different;
- URL/reference;
- access status;
- tier;
- geographic scope;
- official/media/supporting;
- claim supported;
- conflict note.

## 5. Verification standard

| Item type | Minimum standard |
|---|---|
| Material corporate action | Official source preferred; media alone remains unconfirmed/planned as appropriate |
| Material policy/project status | Official source or at least two credible independent sources if official source unavailable |
| Routine sector radar item | One credible accessible source, with confidence adjusted |
| International item with local implication | Credible international source + explicit transmission-path analysis; local verification when claim concerns Indonesia |
| Rumor | Label rumor; never elevate based on repetition |

## 6. Access limitations

If paywalled, blocked, or inaccessible:

1. do not infer content from title/snippet;
2. note the access limitation;
3. find accessible alternatives;
4. lower confidence if verification remains incomplete;
5. exclude the item if its core claim cannot be responsibly described.

## 7. Conflict rules

- show conflicting values/statuses separately;
- attach source and date to each;
- official source governs formal status when relevant;
- do not average or choose by intuition;
- mark information gap and next verification step.

## 8. Copyright

Paraphrase and synthesize. Do not reproduce full articles or create a substitute
for paid content. Direct quotation must be short and necessary.

## 9. Sources not accepted as factual basis

Anonymous blogs, unattributed aggregators, unverified social posts, forums,
message groups, and leaked/non-public material. They may generate a search lead
but not a factual report item.

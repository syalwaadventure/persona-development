# Rekind Sector Intelligence Brief — Mode K Guide

## 1. Hard sector boundary

Mode K covers only:

1. Energi & Ketenagalistrikan;
2. Oil & Gas / Migas;
3. Petrokimia;
4. Pupuk / Fertilizer.

Do not add other sectors without explicit instruction. A related story must have
a direct link to one of these four sectors.

## 2. Sector taxonomy and search dimensions

| Sector | Included subsectors/topics | Indonesian search terms | English search terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Energy & Power | generation, grid, IPP, renewables, solar, geothermal, electricity, steam/utilities | energi, listrik, pembangkit, PLN, EBT, IPP, PLTS, panas bumi, steam, boiler | power, electricity, power plant, grid, renewable energy, solar, geothermal, IPP, steam, boiler |
| Oil & Gas | upstream, downstream, refinery, LNG, LPG, gas processing, storage, pipeline | migas, hulu, hilir, kilang, LNG, LPG, gas, SKK Migas, Pertamina | oil, natural gas, upstream, downstream, refinery, LNG, LPG, gas processing, pipeline |
| Petrochemical | feedstock, naphtha, olefin, polymer, methanol, industrial chemical | petrokimia, nafta, olefin, polimer, metanol, kimia industri | petrochemical, naphtha, olefin, polymer, methanol, industrial chemicals |
| Fertilizer | ammonia, urea, NPK, subsidy, feedstock gas, fertilizer plant | pupuk, fertilizer, Pupuk Indonesia, urea, NPK, amonia, subsidi pupuk | fertilizer, ammonia, urea, NPK, fertilizer plant, feedstock gas |

For each sector search across six buckets:

- policy/regulation;
- project/investment/tender;
- corporate action/partnership;
- price/supply/demand/feedstock;
- technology/operations/O&M;
- global development with local transmission path.

## 3. Default parameters

| Parameter | Default |
|---|---|
| Period | 24 hours |
| Weekly request | 7 days |
| Sector | four strict sectors |
| Language | Indonesian report; bilingual search |
| Output | standalone HTML |
| Target depth | 6–8 minute read when enough material exists |

| International sources | CNBC International/CNBC.com plus credible global sources |

## 4. Candidate selection gate

Include only if:

- directly in scope;
- current or has a meaningful status change;
- source is credible and accessible;
- contains a concrete development, not generic commentary;
- has direct or explainable relevance to Indonesia/Rekind;
- is not duplicate;
- can be described without speculation.

## 5. Relevance scoring

### HIGH

Direct implications for Indonesian project pipeline, EPC tender, investment,
Pupuk Indonesia Group, Rekind, O&M, commissioning, utilities, industrial
trading, major input cost, or regulation.

### MEDIUM

Indirect but credible implication through commodity/feedstock price, technology,
regional supply-demand, or competitor investment.

### LOW

Useful background but no near-term action or direct project/business link. LOW
items should be limited and removed first when the brief is long.

## 6. Global-to-local transmission path

Every international item must state:

`Global event → affected commodity/technology/company/capacity → regional/Indonesia channel → potential relevance to four sectors/Rekind`.

Acceptable channels:

- commodity or feedstock price;
- supply availability;
- equipment lead time;
- technology adoption;
- financing/capex;
- project delay or acceleration;
- policy/regulatory response;
- competitor or partner behavior;
- demand for EPC, O&M, commissioning, utilities, or industrial trading.

If no credible path exists, exclude the item.

## 7. Deep item analysis

Each item includes:

1. headline;
2. what changed;
3. event/status;
4. key verified facts;
5. sector and value-chain position;
6. why it matters for Rekind;
7. transmission path;
8. affected stakeholders;
9. horizon;
10. opportunity/risk signal;
11. next indicator to monitor;
12. sources and confidence.

Do not inflate low-information stories into long analysis.

## 8. Read-time estimate

Estimate from visible report words, excluding HTML/CSS/JS.

Use 180–220 words/minute and display a range. Target 6–8 minutes only when
material content supports it. Quiet days may be 2–4 minutes.

## 9. Output structure

- header and coverage metadata;
- executive signals;
- sector dashboard;
- section per sector with material items;
- global-to-Indonesia transmission table;
- opportunity/risk matrix;
- scan table;
- upcoming indicators;
- source coverage and limitations;
- disclaimer.

## 10. Visual rules

Use inline CSS/SVG only. No external font, chart library, script, or CDN.
Required visual aids:

- sector item-count bar;
- relevance badges;
- executive-signal cards;
- opportunity/risk matrix or impact-horizon matrix.

## 11. Anti-filler rules

- Skip sectors without material news.
- Do not repeat the same fact in summary, sector card, and table with long text.
- Avoid generic macro commentary.
- Do not force equal item counts per sector.
- International source coverage is not a quota.

# Analysis Guide

## 1. Corporate action analysis

For each event answer:

1. What changed?
2. What is the latest verified status?
3. Who are the parties and assets?
4. What is the transaction structure?
5. What strategic rationale is stated or inferred?
6. What material values are verified?
7. Which approvals/conditions remain?
8. What is the timeline?
9. Who is affected?
10. What execution risks exist?
11. What is unknown?
12. What is the next milestone?

Separate:

- verified fact;
- company statement;
- media claim;
- analyst inference;
- information gap.

## 2. Sector intelligence analysis

For Mode K, analyze only within the four-sector boundary.

### A. Event layer

- latest development;
- event date vs publication date;
- status and evidence;
- parties, project/asset, geography;
- verified numbers.

### B. Sector layer

- sector/subsector;
- value-chain position;
- supply-demand effect;
- commodity/feedstock effect;
- policy/regulatory effect;
- technology/operations effect.

### C. Business relevance layer

- EPC project/tender pipeline;
- engineering/procurement/construction implication;
- commissioning/O&M/maintenance implication;
- utilities/industrial trading implication;
- partner/competitor/client implication;
- capital, schedule, or supply-chain implication.

### D. Transmission layer for international news

`global event → sector mechanism → Indonesia channel → Rekind relevance`.

Do not skip this layer. If it cannot be demonstrated, exclude the story.

### E. Time horizon

- Immediate: 0–3 months;
- Near term: 3–12 months;
- Medium/long term: >12 months.

### F. Monitoring layer

State next observable signal, such as official approval, FID, tender, contract
award, financial close, construction start, commissioning, price threshold,
regulatory issuance, or company disclosure.

## 3. Analysis wording

Use “may/could/potential” only when accompanied by mechanism and uncertainty.
Avoid generic lines such as “this creates opportunities for Rekind” without
explaining the activity, client, project, or value-chain pathway.
